Download & Install
Less than 2 minutes — Windows 10/11 only for now
01
Download the installer
Go to roundclear.in/download.html → click Download for Windows. File: RoundClearAI_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe (~90 MB).

Open the downloaded file from your Downloads folder.

First Time Setup
Fill these in before starting your session
01
Company Name
Enter the company you're interviewing at — e.g. TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, HCL. The AI tailors answers to that company's interview style.
02
Your Target Role
Type your role — e.g. Security Engineer, DevOps Engineer, Java Developer, Data Analyst. This changes the depth and focus of every answer.
03
Round Type
Select Technical, HR, or Coding.
Technical — deep technical answers with tools and mechanisms
HR — behavioral answers in STAR format
Coding — logic explanation with time complexity
04
Upload Resume (optional but recommended)
Upload your resume as PDF or Word file. The app extracts your tech stack to improve transcription accuracy — tools like Kubernetes or CrowdStrike in your resume won't get misheared.
💡 Always upload your resume — it noticeably improves answer relevance and transcription quality.

3
During Your Interview
Step by step while the interview is live
01
Start session 5 minutes before
Open RoundClear AI → fill in company, role, round → upload resume → select audio mode → click Start Free Session. Do this before the interviewer joins — not while they're waiting.
02
App listens automatically
No button to press. The app listens continuously. As soon as the interviewer finishes speaking, the question is transcribed and sent to the AI automatically.
03
Glance at KEY POINTS first
KEY POINTS are 4 bullet facts at the top of every answer. Read them in 3 seconds to get the core concept before you start speaking.
04
Speak the SPEAK THIS paragraph
SPEAK THIS is written exactly how you should say it aloud. Read it naturally at a comfortable pace. Simple questions give ~100 words, deep technical questions give ~200 words, scenario questions give ~250 words.
💡 Don't read robotically word for word. Glance at a sentence, look up, speak it naturally. Small variations are fine and actually sound more confident.
05
Check FOLLOW-UP READY
After each answer, 3 likely follow-up questions are shown. Use the gap while the interviewer processes your answer to mentally prepare for what's coming next.
06
Watch the timer bar
The top bar shows remaining free time — 30 minutes on the free plan. When it hits zero the payment screen appears.
⚠️ A typical interview is 45–60 minutes. If you're expecting a long session, upgrade to the 120 min pack before your interview starts — not mid-session.

Common Issues & Fixes
Quick answers to the most frequent problems
App is not picking up audio from Zoom / Teams+
Make sure you selected System Audio mode — not Microphone. System Audio captures sound through your speakers or headphones. Also check that Windows volume is not muted and the video call audio is actually playing through the computer (not a phone).
Answer is not showing or takes too long+
Almost always a slow internet connection. The app sends audio to Deepgram Nova-3 for transcription, then to Claude for answer generation — both need a stable connection. Use WiFi, not mobile hotspot, during interviews. If consistently slow, check your backend is still running on Railway.
Question was transcribed incorrectly+
Deepgram Nova-3 can occasionally mishear technical terms. To improve: upload your resume (adds your tech stack as hints to the transcription), ensure the interviewer's audio is clear, and avoid speaking over each other. If one question comes through wrong — wait for the next one, don't try to correct it mid-interview.
Windows blocked the app or shows "Unknown Publisher"+
Click "More Info" on the SmartScreen popup → then "Run Anyway". The app is safe. This warning appears because we are an independent developer without a code signing certificate (these cost thousands of rupees). We are working on getting this for a future version.
Payment went through but minutes were not added+
This can happen if the app crashed right after payment. Email support@roundclear.in with your Dodo payment ID from your payment confirmation email and we will manually activate your minutes within 24 hours.
Can I use RoundClear AI on Mac?+
Yes — Mac is supported. Download the .dmg from roundclear.in/download.html. On Mac, use earphones during online interviews so the interviewer's voice is captured clearly.
Is my audio recorded or stored anywhere?+
No. Audio is processed in real-time and immediately discarded after transcription. We do not store any recordings. The transcribed text is passed to Claude for answer generation only and is not logged or saved on our servers.
The app window disappeared during my interview+
Click the RoundClear AI icon in your Windows taskbar at the bottom of the screen. You can also press Alt + Tab to switch back to it. Before starting your next interview, keep the app window visible on the side of your screen so it doesn't get buried.
