🔒 Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

RoundClear AI ("RoundClear", "we", "us") builds a real-time interview coaching application. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website (roundclear.in) and our desktop application.

1. What We Collect

DataWhenWhy
Email addressGoogle sign-in (in-app, only when unlocking paid minutes)Link your purchased minutes to your account, across devices
Device identifierAutomatically, on first app launchEnforce the 30-minute free trial per device
Session usage (minutes used)During active sessionsTrack remaining balance, enforce limits
Payment confirmationWhen you purchase minutesUnlock your paid minutes; we never see or store your card details — payments are handled entirely by our processor, Dodo Payments

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not record, store, or retain your interview audio. Audio is streamed for real-time transcription and discarded immediately after processing.
  • We do not store transcripts of your interview conversations after your session ends.
  • We do not store your resume or any documents you may reference — nothing is uploaded or persisted by the app for this purpose.
  • We never store your payment card, UPI, or bank details — these are handled exclusively by Dodo Payments' PCI-compliant systems.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

To provide the product, we rely on the following processors, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Google (OAuth) — for account sign-in
  • Supabase — authentication and account/session data storage
  • AssemblyAI — real-time speech-to-text transcription (audio, not retained by us afterward)
  • Anthropic (Claude) — generates suggested answers from the transcribed question text
  • Dodo Payments — payment processing
  • Railway, Cloudflare — hosting infrastructure for our backend and website

4. How We Use Your Data

We use the data described above solely to operate the product: authenticating you, tracking your remaining session minutes, processing payments, and generating real-time interview answers. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Data Retention

Account and balance data (email, remaining minutes, payment history) is retained for as long as your account is active, so your purchased minutes remain available across devices and sessions. You can request deletion of your account data at any time by contacting us below.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, email us at support@roundclear.in.

7. Children's Privacy

RoundClear is not directed at, and should not be used by, anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@roundclear.in.
