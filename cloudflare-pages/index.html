Can the interviewer detect RoundClear?
No. RoundClear runs as a separate desktop app on your screen. It doesn't inject into Zoom or Teams, doesn't modify audio streams, and is completely invisible to the interviewer. Just keep the window off-camera.
Does it work on Mac?
Yes — a native Mac .dmg is available on the download page. On Mac, RoundClear uses the microphone instead of system audio, so use earphones during online interviews so the interviewer's voice is captured by your mic.
Does it work for non-tech roles — marketing, finance, HR?
Yes — every domain is supported. Select your role in the setup screen and RoundClear tailors answers to your field. 12+ professional domains covered.
What if I reinstall or get a new laptop?
Your paid minutes are linked to your email. Open the app, enter your email in the Plan section and click "Recover my minutes" — your remaining balance is restored instantly.
What happens after 120 minutes?
Simply buy another 120-minute pack inside the app. There's no subscription — you pay only when you need minutes. Minutes never expire once purchased.
Is my data private?
Audio is processed in real-time and immediately discarded — we don't store recordings or transcripts. Your resume is used only to personalise answers for your active session. Nothing is stored on our servers.
